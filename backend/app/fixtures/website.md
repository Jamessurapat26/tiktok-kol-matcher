# ExampleBrand

ExampleBrand is a Bangkok-based eyewear brand making blue-light glasses and
screen-friendly frames for gamers, students and desk workers.

## What we make

- **Blue-light glasses** with a light-filtering lens for long screen sessions.
- **Gaming glasses** with a wide, lightweight frame that fits under headsets.
- **Everyday screen eyewear** for office work, studying and content creation.
- **Clip-on blue-light lenses** for people who already wear prescription glasses.

## Who we are for

Our audience is mostly 16–34 year olds in Thailand who game, stream, study or
work on screens every day. We also ship to gamers across Southeast Asia.

## Why ExampleBrand

- Lightweight frames designed for long gaming sessions.
- Blue-light filtering lenses that keep colours natural on screen.
- Local Bangkok brand with fast delivery across Thailand.
- Friendly, gamer-coded tone: practical reviews, setup content and real use cases.

## Content we like

Short-form reviews, "day in the life of a gamer" clips, desk setup tours,
screen-time tips, eyewear styling and honest before/after comparisons. We work
with nano, micro, macro and mega creators who can show the product in real life.
